How it works

Write a rule.It stops the request.You see that it did.

Scene 01 · You say it once

You write one sentence.

Plain words, straight into the console.

Warden turns it into rules.

Concrete prohibitions, compiled with the examples they must catch.

You activate them, one by one.

Two go live. One you leave off. Nothing judges anyone until you say so.

Write the set
r-customer-personal-datablockCustomer personal data — names together with contact details, addresses, or document numbers — must not be exported or prepared for any recipient outside the company.activated
r-contract-termsblockContract terms negotiated with a specific customer must not be disclosed to, or compared for, any other customer.skipped
r-payment-approvalescalateNo one may approve, authorise, or instruct the release of a payment above USD 5,000 without written sign-off from the CEO.activated
