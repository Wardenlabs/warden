# Banned words and phrases

Never use these. Replace with something concrete, or restructure the sentence.

## Verbs and verb phrases
delve, delve into, dive into, unpack, navigate (figurative), leverage, harness,
utilize, foster, empower, elevate, unlock, embark, embark on a journey,
underscore, highlight (as "emphasise"), showcase, spearhead, streamline,
revolutionize, transform (as filler), curate, craft (as "make"), weave,
illuminate, shed light on, resonate, align (figurative), catalyze, amplify,
supercharge, turbocharge, demystify, reimagine, redefine

## Nouns
landscape (figurative), realm, tapestry, journey (figurative), ecosystem
(non-biological), synergy, paradigm, framework (as filler), cornerstone,
game-changer, game changer, deep dive, treasure trove, wealth of, plethora,
myriad, testament, beacon, bedrock, backbone (figurative), nuance (as filler),
toolkit (as filler), north star, blueprint (as filler)

## Adjectives
seamless, robust, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, groundbreaking, innovative,
transformative, unparalleled, unprecedented, invaluable, pivotal, crucial (as
filler), vital (as filler), comprehensive, holistic, multifaceted, intricate,
compelling, remarkable, profound, meticulous, vibrant, dynamic (as filler),
bespoke, curated, rich (figurative), powerful (as filler), significant (as
filler), key (as filler)

## Openers and connectives
In today's fast-paced world, In today's digital age, In the ever-evolving
world of, It's important to note that, It's worth noting that, It is crucial
to understand, Let's explore, Let's dive in, Whether you're a ... or a ...,
At its core, Ultimately, Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally (as filler),
That said, Needless to say, In conclusion, To sum up, In summary (in short
pieces), Here's the thing, The truth is, Simply put

## Closers
I hope this helps, Feel free to reach out, Don't hesitate to, The possibilities
are endless, The future is bright, Only time will tell, One thing is clear,
At the end of the day, When all is said and done

## Constructions
"It's not just X, it's Y", "not only ... but also", "isn't just about X. It's
about Y", "more than just", "X meets Y" (as a title), "the intersection of",
"a symphony of", "a masterclass in", "Think of it as", "Enter: X",
"Say goodbye to X", "Gone are the days"

## Hedges used as filler
arguably, essentially, fundamentally, effectively (as filler), quite possibly,
some might say, in many ways, to a certain extent

## Emphasis tics
truly, deeply, incredibly, absolutely, genuinely, undoubtedly, certainly,
definitely, literally (when not literal), very (as filler)
