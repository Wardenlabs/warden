Download the on-device models

Warden judges every prompt locally, so nothing leaves this machine. That needs ~5.4 GB of model weights, fetched once from HuggingFace and kept in your app data folder.

Demo mode runs the whole console against a keyword-matching test double, so you can look around, but it guards nothing and no verdict it shows is evidence about anything. Download the models later from the Gateway menu, or just drop them in: the app picks them up on the next start.
